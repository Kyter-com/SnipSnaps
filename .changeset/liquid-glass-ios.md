Refresh the interface for the latest iOS and macOS design language: Liquid Glass buttons, review controls, and info chips on iPhone and iPad; a floating tab bar that tucks away while scrolling; pull-to-refresh photo counts on Home; and livelier tap feedback across review cards.

Also fixes two small issues the refresh surfaced: the photo permission prompt now waits for the Enable button instead of firing at launch, and the Home sort pills no longer show a doubled chevron on macOS 27.

On the Mac Files tab, the REVIEW categories now sit above the folder lists so they stay visible no matter how many folders are added.
